A comprehensive study guide, beginning with prerequisite review and building through standard and measure-theoretic definitions, joint-to-marginal-to-conditional derivations, and $(E[Y\mid X=x]$). It also covers conditional CDFs, total expectation, total variance, Bayes reversal, and the best-predictor interpretation.

The **six fully worked problems** cover:
- **Discrete joint table:** Conditional probabilities, moments, and reversal.
- **Continuous square density:** Conditional CDF and nonlinear conditional mean.
- **Piecewise triangular support:** Integration bounds and variance decomposition.
- **Beta-binomial model:** Mixed discrete–continuous conditioning.
- **Bivariate normal:** Full derivation by completing the square.
- **Singular distribution:** Conditional laws without a joint PDF.

The guide includes lecture-prep questions, common mistakes, self-tests with answers, and a compact reference sheet.

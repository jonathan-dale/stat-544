## Poisson Processes
Graduate-level guide for Poisson Porcess following a similiar detail and structure of the other guides. Each includes prerequisites, derivations, worked examples, practice problems with solutions, reference formulas, and executable R checks.

#### Poisson Process I:
Definitions, independent increments, forward equations, exponential waiting times, gamma arrival times, joint distributions, covariance, and optional martingale material.

#### Poisson Process II:
Conditional binomial and multinomial counts, uniform order statistics, spacings, thinning, superposition, and competing streams, plus clearly labeled nonhomogeneous, compound-process, and inference extensions.
